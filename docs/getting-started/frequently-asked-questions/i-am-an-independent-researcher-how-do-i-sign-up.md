# I am an Independent Researcher, how do I sign up?

We've received many emails from concerned researchers who want to sign up quickly but aren't affiliated with an academic institution. Please do not use an old role or affiliation as your current position, as that may delay moderation.

If you are not affiliated with an academic institution, but are employed at a company, you can use that domain and position information to sign up. This is recommended if at all possible.

If you do not have a company domain, you can use a personal email. All personal emails will be moderated - see [Expediting Profile Activation](../creating-an-openreview-profile/expediting-profile-activation.md) for additional tips on how to make sure your profile is activated as quickly as possible.

When filling out the profile details for registration, there is a section that will ask for your Career & Education History, which will require a current position. For the Position field select **'Independent Researcher'**, which will lock the Institution Info domains automatically. See the page [Entering Institutional Data](../creating-an-openreview-profile/entering-institutional-data.md) for an example of how to fill out institutional information as an independent researcher. Please include as much of your background as possible. If the 'Career & Education History' section only lists 'Independent Researcher' your profile will be rejected with a request to add another institution record.

If you are not affiliated with an institution, set your current position to **Independent Researcher**. When you select this position, OpenReview automatically fills in the institution domain (`independent-researcher.org`) and disables the Institution Domain and Institution Name fields — you don't need to enter them yourself.&#x20;

Because personal emails are moderated, adding and confirming an institutional email is still the fastest way to get your profile activated.
