# Information for high school students

OpenReview welcomes younger researchers, aged 13 and above, to participate in scholarly peer review.&#x20;

\
No one under the age of 13 may create or hold an OpenReview account. If OpenReview learns that an account holder is under 13, the account will be suspended and the associated personal data is deleted in accordance with the [Privacy Policy](https://openreview.net/legal/privacy).

\
If you are at least 13 and under 18 years of age, you must have the consent of a parent or legal guardian. Please have your parent or legal guardian complete and sign the form, then upload it as a PDF to your profile with the unique link sent to you in the temporary rejection message. If you do not receive the link, please submit a message through the [Contact Form](https://openreview.net/contact).&#x20;



A parent or guardian may withdraw consent at any time. Upon request, accounts can be closed and data will be deleted.&#x20;
