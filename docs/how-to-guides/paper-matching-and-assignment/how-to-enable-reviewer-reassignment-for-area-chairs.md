# How to enable Reviewer Reassignment for Area Chairs

By default, only the Program Chairs and Senior Area Chairs can change reviewer assignments. You can let Area Chairs modify the reviewer assignments of the submissions they are assigned to, either the deployed assignments or a proposed assignment that has not been deployed yet.

Before you enable it, make sure Area Chairs are assigned to submissions, and that reviewer assignments are deployed or a proposed assignment exists.

## Enable reviewer reassignment

1. Access the Venue Timeline, go to the **Area Chairs** group and click on the **Reviewer Reassignment** button.
2. Fill in the form:
   * **Enable Reviewers Reassignment**: set to **True**.
   * **Reviewers Name**: the reviewer group whose assignments the Area Chairs can edit. Change it only if your venue has more than one reviewer group.
   * **Reviewers Proposed Assignment Title**: to let Area Chairs edit a proposed assignment, enter the title of that matching. Leave it empty to let them edit the deployed assignments.
3. Click **Submit**.

If your venue has more than one Area Chair group, each group has its own **Reviewer Reassignment** option.

## What Area Chairs see

The Area Chair Console shows a **Modify Reviewer Assignments** link. It opens the assignment browser for the Area Chair's submissions, for proposed assignments if you entered a matching title, or for deployed assignments otherwise.

<figure><img src="https://622636955-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FVorH499wd7ipUjYX5etp%2Fuploads%2F1H8JfDLBPwJkW81BjjxW%2Fimage.png?alt=media&#x26;token=b53077eb-f2d0-495d-a4a4-3f4174eb692b" alt="The top of the Area Chairs Console, with the line Reviewer Assignment Browser: Modify Reviewer Assignments below the console description."><figcaption><p>The link Area Chairs use to modify reviewer assignments.</p></figcaption></figure>

When you deploy proposed assignments, the matching title is removed from the form and the link opens the deployed assignments instead. Reassignment stays enabled.

## Disable reviewer reassignment

Open the same form, set **Enable Reviewers Reassignment** to **False**, and click **Submit**. The link no longer appears in the Area Chair Console.
