# How to Enable Commenting on Submissions

Venues created with the Conference Review Workflow include an **Official Comment** step in the [Workflow Timeline](../../getting-started/hosting-a-venue-on-openreview/navigating-your-venue-pages.md#workflow-timeline). By default, commenting opens 4 weeks after the submission deadline (the full submission deadline, if your venue has one) and closes 6 weeks after it. The Program Chairs, assigned Senior Area Chairs, assigned Area Chairs, assigned reviewers, and the submission's authors can post comments, and the person commenting chooses who can read each comment.

To change when and how commenting is enabled, click **Official Comment** in the Workflow Timeline to expand the step, then use the **Edit** link next to each control and click **Submit**.

## Set when commenting is open

Edit **Dates**:

* **Activation Date** – when the comment form becomes available on each submission.
* **Expiration Date** – when the comment form is no longer available.

## Choose who can comment and who can read comments

Edit **Writers And Readers**:

* **Writers** – "Who should be able to participate in this stage (read and write comments)?" Choose from Program Chairs, Assigned Senior Area Chairs, Assigned Area Chairs, Assigned Reviewers, Assigned Reviewers who already submitted their review, and Submission Authors. Program Chairs are always included.
* **Readers** – "Who should be able to only read comments?" These are the reader options commenters can choose from. Program Chairs are always included.

Senior Area Chair and Area Chair options only appear if your venue has those roles.

## Choose who is notified

Edit **Notifications** and select which groups are emailed when a comment is posted. By default, assigned reviewers and submission authors are notified. An email is only sent to a selected user if they are also a reader of the comment.

## Change the comment form

Edit **Form Fields** to add, change, or remove fields of the comment form. See [How to modify the Review, Meta Review, and Decision Forms](how-to-modify-the-review-meta-review-and-decision-forms.md) for how the form editor works.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Public** is not one of the reader options of the **Official Comment** step, and comments from users outside your venue's committee (public comments) cannot be turned on from the Workflow Timeline. To allow either, contact OpenReview Support by posting a comment on your [venue request form](../../getting-started/hosting-a-venue-on-openreview/navigating-your-venue-pages.md#venue-request-form).
{% endhint %}
