# How to Enable an Ethics Review Stage

If your venue needs ethics review, contact OpenReview Support by adding a comment to your [venue request form](../../getting-started/hosting-a-venue-on-openreview/navigating-your-venue-pages.md#venue-request-form). Include:

* whether you will have Ethics Chairs, Ethics Reviewers, or both,
* how submissions will be flagged for ethics review,
* the dates for the ethics review period.

We recommend reaching out well before the ethics review period starts.
