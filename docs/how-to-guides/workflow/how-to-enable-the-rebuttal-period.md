# How to Enable the Rebuttal Period

Venues created with the Conference Review Workflow include an **Author Rebuttal** step in the [Workflow Timeline](../../getting-started/hosting-a-venue-on-openreview/navigating-your-venue-pages.md#workflow-timeline). Authors can post one rebuttal per submission, as a reply to the submission. By default, the rebuttal form opens 5.5 weeks after the submission deadline (the full submission deadline, if your venue has one) and is due 6.5 weeks after it.

Before the rebuttal period starts, make sure authors have [access to the reviews](how-to-release-reviews-decisions-and-metareviews.md). By default, the **Official Review Release** step releases reviews to the authors 5 weeks after the submission deadline.

## Set the rebuttal dates

1. From the Workflow Timeline, click **Author Rebuttal** to expand the step.
2. Next to **Dates**, click **Edit**.
3. Set the **Activation Date** (when authors can start posting rebuttals), the **Due Date** (the deadline shown to authors), and the **Expiration Date** (when the rebuttal form is no longer available).
4. Click **Submit**.

## Other rebuttal settings

The **Author Rebuttal** step has these other controls, each with an **Edit** link:

* **Readers** – who can read the rebuttals. By default, the Program Chairs, the assigned Senior Area Chairs, Area Chairs, and reviewers, and the submission's authors.
* **Notifications** – who is emailed when a rebuttal is posted. By default, the assigned reviewers and the submission's authors. An email is only sent to a selected user if they are also a reader of the rebuttal.
* **Form Fields** – the fields of the rebuttal form. See [How to modify the Review, Meta Review, and Decision Forms](how-to-modify-the-review-meta-review-and-decision-forms.md) for how the form editor works.

{% hint style="info" %}
If you do not want a rebuttal period, click **Disable** next to the **Author Rebuttal** step before its activation date. A disabled step does not create the rebuttal forms, and you can turn it back on with **Enable**.
{% endhint %}
